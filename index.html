The problem

Closets are built for someone else’s clothes.

A builder’s closet is one rail and one shelf, hung at a height no one chose. Everything you own is folded, stacked, or on the floor, and the room you paid for is the one you never show. A wardrobe room starts from what you own and the way you dress, and then it is built to the sixteenth of an inch.

Process

Measured on Tuesday.
Standing by Friday evening.

  1. Measure

    We visit, measure the room, and count what you hang, fold and store. Nothing is designed until it is counted.

    In your home · 60 minutes

  2. Design

    A rendered plan of the frame, rails and drawers, with a fixed price. One revision round is included.

    Within one week

  3. Build

    Frames are welded and drawers built at our bench in New Orleans. You see photos before anything ships.

    Our shop · 3 to 4 weeks

  4. Install

    Carried in flat, assembled in place. Most rooms are finished, lit and cleaned up in one day.

    One day · most rooms

The system

Three parts. Any room.

I

The open frame

Bronze-finished steel, welded square, fixed floor to ceiling. No carcass, no back panel, so the room stays a room.

II

Oak drawers

Solid white oak fronts on dovetailed boxes and soft-close runners. Sized to your folds, not to a catalogue.

III

Lit rails

Warm LED built into every rail, so you can see navy from black at seven in the morning.
